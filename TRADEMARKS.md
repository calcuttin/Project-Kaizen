# Project Kaizen naming and branding

**Project Kaizen is a provisional, placeholder name.** Other companies and projects use similar names in other fields. Before a broader launch, this project will need distinct branding or a different name. No affiliation with those businesses is implied.

The AGPL license grants rights to the source code, not to product names, logos, or visual marks used to identify the official hosted Kaizen service.

Forks may state that they are based on Kaizen and must make their modifications available as required by the AGPL. Forks should use their own product name and logo unless they have written permission to present themselves as an official Kaizen service.
